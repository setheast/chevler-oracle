## Rulings

1. **The mother reel decides the reel-fed route (Hannah, 27 Oct 2026).**
   - **The case:** 51 × 38 round cases in 50gsm GP Kraft go on **RF2 (single head), not 10RF (twin head)**. GP Kraft 50 is bought only as a 960mm mother reel, and 10RF's 263mm web cuts just 3 from it: 171mm, or 17.8%, is offcut. The 1–5REEL die cuts 7 × 137mm lanes (0.1%).
   - **On white GP:** the 1050 reel slits 4 × 263 cleanly, so the same product is right on 10RF.
   - **For Oracle:** route selection must cost the **whole mother reel consumed**, not just the slit width. Material, ink and conversion all apply to the offcut, because the full mother runs through the press/slitter. Route comparison must include it, or twin-head routes look cheaper than they are on a reel that doesn't divide.
2. **Slitting tolerance: up to 3mm over nominal** (Access evidence, 27 Oct 2026; not yet confirmed by production).
   - 132 of 1,161 Access reel products with a mother reel are slit 1–3mm over; 116 are 4 × 263 from 1050.
   - The median offcut across the record is 0% (p90 0.6%). Products are normally chosen to slit cleanly, so the offcut matters mainly when a size moves to another paper.
   - **For Oracle:** lanes = floor((mother + tolerance) ÷ slit width), with the tolerance a calibration parameter. → **new R-item:** confirm 3mm and the 5% flag with Hannah/Stuart.
3. **Materials are chosen by stock line, not description + gsm** (Seth, 27 Oct 2026).
   - The label says what the line is, e.g. `102404 63gsm Black Market Paper (1,200 Mill Reel)`, `(525 × 750 Sheet)` or `(285 × 4,000m Slit Reel)`.
   - The line fixes the price (the workbook priced the first VLOOKUP match, quirk C1) and the mother reel width.
   - **For Oracle:** a quote's material is a Materials entity row (stock code), never a name + gsm key. Mill-reel rows carry the width that drives slitting.
4. **Multi-head die webs must be measured, never derived.**
   - The 51×38 10REEL 2-up die has no web on file (263mm is known from the products' BOMs, WCCR38/YF855), so the sandbox can't offer it as an alternative route.
   - **For Oracle:** a Tool carries its measured web width; the R7 enrichment worklist includes it.
5. **The Cases materials list is a workbook snapshot (13 Aug 2026), not the stock file.** Example: GP Kraft 50 has one line (101759, 960mm), while AX312 historically used a now-dormant 526mm kraft reel. Oracle's Materials should come from the stock file with status (live / dormant / obsolete).

## Sandbox implementation (pointers, not to migrate here)

- `server/engines/cases/shared.ts`:
  - optional `inputs.slitLanes` → a "Mother-reel offcut" build-up line at the piece cost × (1/used − 1), plus `internals.offcut`;
  - optional `inputs.stockCode` → prices that row.
  - Missing = legacy (`offcut-stockline.test.ts`). `weights.ts` grosses bought kg by the offcut.
- `server/estimate-checks.ts`:
  - `slitLayout` (lanes with the tolerance) and `lowerWasteRoutes` (ready dies on running reel formers that slit cleanly; multi-head only with a measured web);
  - checks `mother-offcut` and `offcut-route`.
- `server/production-rules.ts`: `SLIT_OVERWIDTH_TOLERANCE_MM` = 3, `OFFCUT_FLAG_PCT` = 0.05, `REEL_FORMER`.
- `server/cases-material-lines.ts` (pure): `materialLineLabel`, `materialLines`, `resolveMaterialLine`, `lineMotherReelWidth`.
- `server/access-product-map.ts`: the loader pins the matched stock line (or the single mill reel at the live mother width) and turns on auto lanes.
