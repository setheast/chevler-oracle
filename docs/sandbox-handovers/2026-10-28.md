# Oracle hand-over — 28 October 2026

**For:** the Chevler Oracle build. Fold into the quoting-module spec (§2 rulings, §5).
**Covers:** production-knowledge §26 (Seth).

## Rulings

1. **Print plates by press** (Seth, 28 Oct 2026).
   - Reel-Sheet: stereo or cylinder. Narrow Reeler: stereo or cylinder. Wide Reeler: cylinder only.
   - A solid print needs a cylinder (a stereo's joint line shows — Stuart, 27 Oct). It can run on any press that takes a cylinder, not only the Wide Reeler (refines ruling 37).
   - **For Oracle:** the Print Tools register holds plate type per press capability; origination is priced per press × plate × colour, and each job/quote records the plate chosen.
2. **An un-costed option is never compared as free.** A route whose plate has no price is excluded from the route comparison (with the reason) until the price is set. This is doctrine for every assumption-engine comparison.

## Later on 28 Oct: litho plates

3. **Litho Printer takes a PLATE, £50 per colour** (Seth, 28 Oct 2026).
   - Litho is quoted only in the Ecoluxe calculator today (off-limits here), but Oracle quotes every product family through one engine.
   - **For Oracle:** the Print Tools register's plate types are stereo / cylinder / plate; origination for litho = plates × colours. The sandbox carries it as a provision: `PRESS_PLATES["Litho Printer"] = ["plate"]`, `lithoPlateCostPerColour` £50.
   - **Reconcile with Ecoluxe:** check whether its own "origination" charge (Live Calc parameters) is the same £50/plate concept before porting → new R-item.

## Later on 28 Oct: Stuart on the Barking Bakery comparison (S12)

4. **A printed white job uses GP Opaque** (Stuart). Plain white GP is still bought for unprinted work.
   - **For Oracle:** a material rule — print type × colour → material grade, applied by the assumption engine when the paper is chosen and shown with its certainty.
5. **Pack quantities appear on a customer quote only when certain:** a previously run job, or the same packing as something the customer already buys. Otherwise they're left off, or given as an estimate on request.
   - **For Oracle:** the quote prints packing from a Confirmed packing standard or the product record only. Suggestions stay internal.
6. **Speed bands need a small-blank end.** A 31 × 23 (83mm blank) runs ~60–70/min against the 55 band for blanks to 100mm (Stuart: "maybe go 60").
   - **For Oracle:** speed standards per machine group × blank band, maintained by production; R14/R8 still apply.

## Later on 28 Oct: rulings 4–6 built in the sandbox

- **4 (GP Opaque):** applied automatically on printed white Greaseproof (`printedPaperSwap`, `cases-material-lines.ts`; rule table `PRINTED_PAPER_SWAPS`). Same-width Opaque line, else the next wider; never a pre-printed line. Recorded per quote (`paperAuto`, `paperSwap`) with a "kept white by hand" note when overridden.
  - **For Oracle:** the material rule is an assumption with an undo, recorded on the quote; blocked where the target grade isn't stocked at that weight.
- **5 (packing on the customer quote):** the bar is a setting (`packingOnQuoteMinCertainty`, seed 0.9). 100% sources: packing as run on a product, a Confirmed standard, or the estimator's "packing confirmed" with a note. The decision (`cases.packingOnQuote`) is stored on the quote; older quotes print as issued.
  - **For Oracle:** packing certainty belongs to the quote line; "customer already buys this packing" should come from the customer's product history (needs customer on products) rather than a tick.
- **6 (small-blank speed):** each RF speed band now carries `status` (confirmed / provisional) and `source`. A provisional band (to 85mm → 60) is Evidenced, and a faster speed against it is a steer, not an error.
  - **For Oracle:** speed standards carry their own certainty; only confirmed ceilings block.
