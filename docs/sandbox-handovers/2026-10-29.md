# Oracle hand-over: 29 Oct 2026

Findings from the sandbox (chevler-pricing-calculator) that Oracle must adopt. Detail: production-knowledge §30.

## Rulings

1. **Delivery comes out of the per-1,000 and goes on its own quote line** (Seth, 29 Oct 2026). The per-1,000 is the making price; delivery = the job's pallets × the 3PL's rate for the delivery postcode zone and service, + a delivery margin (15%, agreed with Lewys 15 Oct).
2. **3PL storage, handling and picking are a cost of the goods, inside the making price** (Seth, 29 Oct 2026). They apply to every job the 3PL stores, delivered or collected (collected jobs paid none of it under the old 4.5%), and earn the normal margin.
   - **RH&D (receipt, handling and despatch) covers a pallet's handling in and out:** once per pallet space, never per delivery, never in the delivery line.
   - **Picking** only where boxes are broken out of pallets (split deliveries); full pallets in and out aren't picked.
3. **What the old 4.5% covered** (Lewys, 29 Oct 2026): all transport, storage and despatch, chosen because those costs have run at 4–5% of revenue for ten years. It also carried sales rebates, customs and some consumables, and those need a home of their own once the % goes.
4. **Rates are dated:** the delivery card (PlasPac: to 31 Oct, then 1 Nov–31 Dec 2026) and storage/handling (current, then 1 Dec 2026) are each picked by the quote date. A changeover switch, Current (the %) / New (the delivery line), shows both side by side on every quote.

## For Oracle

- **Entities:**
  - a **3PL rates** entity with effective dates: delivery cards (postcode-zone coverage × service × consignment size: quarter/half/1–5/per pallet 5+; surcharges) and handling rates (storage/pallet/week, RH&D/pallet, picking/box);
  - a policy: delivery margin, default service, part-pallet limits, dwell.
- **On the job:** a **pallet build** (units per carton × cartons per pallet; the carton is the natural owner, production-knowledge §15), a delivery postcode and service, and a call-off count.
- **The job's delivery line:** quoted cost, margin and charge, kept separately from the making price, so "are we making money on deliveries?" has an answer.
- **Every actual delivery recorded against its job** (OTIF is poor; extra next/same-day deliveries aren't recorded today), so quoted vs actual delivery cost per job is visible.
