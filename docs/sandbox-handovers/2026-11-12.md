## Rulings

1. **Flexo sheets are printed from reels only** (Seth, 12 Nov 2026: "Sheets should not be selectable as we do not have a print press that takes sheets, only reels").
   - **The choice:** a sheet job chooses the PAPER (grade + weight), then one of that paper's **mill reels**.
   - **Not offered:**
     - stocked sheets;
     - the workbook's narrow "@ 4000m" slit-reel rows, which the old engine reads as 4,000mm sheets.
   - **Recalled jobs:** a job saved on a stocked sheet still prices exactly as saved, with an advisory check (`stocked-sheet`).
   - **For Oracle:**
     - Product spec = paper.
     - The route chooses the reel (the same split as Cases, ruling 99).
     - Material rows need a form attribute (mill reel / slit reel / sheet), never inferred from a length column.
     - The sheet-printing route takes reel forms only.

2. **The reel is chosen for utilisation, priced all-in** (Lewys, 12 Nov 2026). Lewys aims "as close to 100% as possible".
   - **Most jobs:** 7–8 in 10 fit the 516mm reel well enough, avoiding the Slitter (his "MCE", most cost-effective).
   - **Otherwise:** a wider reel is slit first. In his example, 355 × 354 at 39gsm GP gives 516 poor, 918 77%, 1140 93% and 1315 80%, so he slits the 1140. 39gsm is slit all the time, so it's no burden.
   - **The Slitter** comes on automatically for a reel wider than the Reel-Sheet takes.
   - **Sandbox:**
     - Every mill reel of the paper is priced at its cheapest allowed cut through the full costing (Slitter, set-ups, guillotine, quantity).
     - ★ = cheapest, which is the 1140 for Lewys' job.
     - "Auto: cheapest reel" is on for fresh jobs and off for recalled ones.
   - **For Oracle:** cost each candidate reel from the cost base. A wider reel carries its slitting pass and offcut; the narrow reel carries its poorer utilisation. Then show the ranking. Don't hard-code "516 first".

3. **Better sizes: trim the size to the cutter** (Seth, after the Ecoluxe calc's alternatives; Lewys: 355 → 354 "because our cut length is 354 … otherwise someone would be paying for waste").
   - **The search:** sizes within ±max(50mm, 20%) at 1mm steps, laid out on the paper's mill reels × the allowed cuts for the print type (Random Repeat: 197, 275, 297, 305, 320, 330, 348, 354, 425, 489) × both orientations (never Registered). Utilisation is floored as the engine does.
   - **Top Pick:** the nearest size at ~100% (≥ 99%), smaller first.
   - **Also listed:** the nearest length-only / width-only change, then the nearest size of each other layout. A larger size is listed only when ≥ 5 points better than the best smaller one.
   - **Pricing:** each size is priced on its own reel and cut.
   - **Smallest change** tags the nearest row.
   - **A size change is the customer's to agree.** Nothing changes until the estimator presses Use.
   - **The Top Pick rule is the estimator's option** (Seth, 12 Nov 2026: "make this an option"):
     - **Nearest to 100%** (default): at 355 × 355 / 39gsm, 328 × 354 on the 1315 (99%, −27mm).
     - **Smallest change that helps** (≥ 5 points better, smaller first): Lewys' 355 × 354 (93%).
     - The other rule's pick always stays listed, tagged.
     - The choice is a per-user preference, and the quote records which rule was used.
     - **For Oracle:** a user preference with an org default, and both picks always visible.

4. **UI (Main, all four kinds):**
   - The Cases sections, in order: Product → Layout → Packing → Material → Price → Delivery → Advanced. Layout (cut length, better sizes) sits straight under Product (Seth, 12 Nov 2026), because it answers the size question.
   - The frozen section strip with One at a time / Several.
   - A summary column with the price per quantity, GP / NP, checks and Generate quote.
   - Full Workings follows the same order.
   - **For Oracle:** this is the same UX reference as ruling 94; the merged estimator should keep one layout for both.

## Addendum 2 (later on 12 Nov 2026) — proven machines by print; a mother reel that isn't stocked

9. **Printed jobs are proven by printed history, plain by plain** (Seth, 12 Nov 2026; production-knowledge §34 addendum). CHV-2611-0189, a printed 45 × 25, was sent to 9REEL by 674 orders that were all plain (3-up from a bought 285mm slit reel). Every printed 45 × 25 ran single-head from a 516 reel (4REEL / 5REEL). The single-head routes price about half (£9 vs £17.63 per 1,000 all-in), and the printed precedent sold at £5.73–£7.96 per 1,000 in the last 12 months.
   - **Sandbox:** `provenMachine(…, {printed})`, with `accessPrinted` reading Access Pln = plain. Products with no print type recorded still count, and the product's own history always counts.
   - **For Oracle:** "proven" evidence must match the job's print class (and, in time, the print process), not just size and weight. Refines ruling 63 (proven before price).
10. **A mother reel the paper isn't bought in is a quote check** (`mother-reel-stock`, attention). 0189 was priced on a typed 1,050mm reel; 39gsm GP comes in 516 / 918 / 1,140 / 1,315. **For Oracle:** a mother reel is a stock-item reference, never a free number.
11. **Open with Lewys:** can a bought slit reel be printed on the Narrow Reeler? Oracle's route candidacy for printed work currently takes mill reels only.
